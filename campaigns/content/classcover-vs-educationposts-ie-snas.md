## EducationPosts.ie is a jobs board. ClassCover is a booking platform.

This is the whole distinction, and it is worth being precise about.

A jobs board is a noticeboard. A school posts a vacancy, the listing goes up, and then everyone waits. You scan the listings, put yourself forward, and hope to be picked. There is no way for a school to know who is free today, and no way to confirm anyone on the spot.

A booking platform works the other way around. The school does not post and hope. It opens its own list of trusted subs and SNAs, sees who has marked themselves available, and sends a booking request. You accept with a tap. Cover is confirmed.

**The Irish substitute and SNA pool exists. What is missing is the system that gets the right available person in front of the school in time. That is what a booking platform does.**

Here is how the two models compare for an SNA.

| Capability | Jobs board (EducationPosts.ie) | Booking platform (ClassCover) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Finding schools advertising for SNAs | Daily bookings, the day-to-day cover schools need |
| How schools reach you | You watch listings and apply | Local schools send you booking requests |
| Availability | Schools cannot see if you are free today | You set your availability on a calendar |
| Getting confirmed | Apply and wait to be chosen | Accept with a tap, booking confirmed |
| Your details | Re-check listings and re-apply | Uploaded once, kept on your profile |
| Messaging | Email back and forth | Two-way messaging in the app |

A jobs board waits. A booking platform confirms.

## How SNAs use EducationPosts.ie today

Right now, if you are an SNA after sub work, EducationPosts.ie is where you go. It is a jobs board: schools post there when they are looking for an SNA, and you scan those listings, make contact, and put yourself forward. That is genuinely useful for finding a school and getting on its radar, and for a lot of SNAs it is the main way of spotting who is hiring.

But finding a school that is advertising is a different thing from being booked for the day. Daily bookings are the day-to-day cover a school needs when an SNA is out: someone free this Thursday, booked and confirmed before the morning bell. A jobs board cannot do that. You are the one checking, applying and waiting to hear back, and a school has no way to see who is available today or to book them on the spot. The platform does have a dedicated booking tool, Sub Seeker, but it is open only to Teaching Council-registered teachers, and it was never available to SNAs. The job board is as far as the platform goes for you.

Connecting through the job board is only the introduction. Once a school has found you there, the daily bookings still come the old way: a phone call or a text, when the school happens to need you and happens to think of you. Our survey of how [subs and SNAs get booked in Ireland](/blog/how-subs-and-snas-actually-get-booked-ireland/) found SNAs the most exposed of anyone, leaning on that single channel with no backup when the phone stays quiet. EducationPosts.ie gets you in the door. It does not handle the booking once you are through it.

ClassCover is built for that day-to-day booking. Once local schools have added you to their list and vetted you, cover requests come to you through the app whenever they need someone, instead of resting on whoever they remember to call. You accept with a tap, and every school you have joined can see when you are free, not just the one with your number saved.

## Where Garda vetting fits in

Garda vetting is the part SNAs ask about most, so it is worth being straight about it.

ClassCover does not carry out Garda vetting, verify it, or speed it up. That responsibility stays with the school and the National Vetting Bureau, exactly as it does now. Vetting is also not portable: a disclosure is [issued to the specific organisation that requested it](https://vetting.garda.ie/help/faq), so in most cases you are vetted again by each school or patron body that takes you on. The one exception is within a single Education and Training Board (ETB), which acts as one organisation for its own schools, so vetting done through the ETB carries across that ETB's schools, but not beyond it.

There has been talk of change. The June 2026 [SNA Workforce Development Plan](https://www.rte.ie/documents/news/2026/06/sna-workforce-development-plan.pdf) proposed a centralised, NCSE-led model where an SNA is "vetted once" and recognised across every school, the way a teacher's Teaching Council vetting already works. It is a proposal, not a rule. The wider plan was [rejected by Fórsa members on 1 July 2026](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/0701/1581323-snas-reject-plan/), and it would need legislation, so nothing changes this school year. You are still vetted by each school that takes you on.

Where ClassCover helps is around the vetting, not the vetting itself. A school can add you to its list before your vetting for that school is confirmed, so you are not lost in the gap, then move you onto its bookable list once it comes through. It can request your vetting certificate and documents through the app instead of chasing them by email. And it stores the expiry dates it holds, with a reminder before they lapse, so once a school has vetted you, you stay in its pipeline instead of quietly dropping out of it. For the full picture, see our guide to [Garda vetting for SNAs](/blog/garda-vetting-snas-ireland/).

## What this means for SNAs

For years, most SNAs have had a single channel to work: the phone call from a school that already knows you. That relationship is not going anywhere, and ClassCover does not replace it. Your regular schools stay at the top of your list.

What changes is that you are no longer reachable only when that one phone rings. Set your availability once, and you become visible to other nearby schools that have vetted you, with booking requests coming to you directly. You go from one channel to several. If you are weighing up whether this kind of work suits you, our guide to [building an SNA career in Ireland](/blog/sna-career-ireland/) walks through it in more detail.

## What this means for principals

If you employ SNAs, the practical win is that you stop running two separate systems: one route for teachers and a scramble of phone calls and WhatsApp messages for SNAs. On ClassCover, both sit on the same booking platform. You see who is available, you send a request, and you get a confirmation, without a public listing to the whole county.
