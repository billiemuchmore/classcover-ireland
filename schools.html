Every kind of school

Works the way your school is run.

Irish schools are run in different ways, with different people signing off on who teaches. ClassCover fits around yours.

  • Primary and post-primary schools

    You vet subs and SNAs through your own vetting body, the way you do now. New people wait on a holding list until you confirm in the app that you've vetted them.

  • ETB schools

    Head office approves each sub once, and every school in your ETB books from the same approved list.

  • Special schools and special classes

    Book SNAs as well as substitute teachers, and add notes to a booking so they know the children's needs before they arrive.

Why county by county

ClassCover works best when schools and subs are in the same area.

A booking platform only works when there are enough substitute teachers and SNAs nearby to fill the gaps. That is why Standard opens county by county, once the local network is strong enough for schools to find people in it.

Every school that signs up shows us where demand is strongest. The counties with the most schools on ClassCover are the next to open Standard.
