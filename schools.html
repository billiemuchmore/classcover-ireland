Why county by county

ClassCover works best when schools and subs are in the same area.

A booking platform only works when there are enough substitute teachers and SNAs nearby to fill the gaps. That is why Standard opens county by county, once the local network is strong enough for schools to find people in it.

Every school that signs up shows us where demand is strongest. The counties with the most schools on ClassCover are the next to open Standard.

Frequently asked questions

Any primary, post-primary, or special school in Ireland. Every school in Ireland can start today. ClassCover Standard is open in Cork, Dublin, Galway, Kildare, Limerick, Louth, Meath, Tipperary and Wicklow. Every other county starts on ClassCover Lite, free forever.
By county. If your school is in Cork, Dublin, Galway, Kildare, Limerick, Louth, Meath, Tipperary or Wicklow, you are on the pilot and get ClassCover Standard free until 30 October 2026. Everywhere else, you start on ClassCover Lite, free forever, and Standard opens in your county as enough local substitute teachers and SNAs join.
ClassCover Lite is a free booking tool. Your school uploads its existing list of substitute teachers and SNAs, and you can send booking requests to them through the app. Lite is free permanently, for every Irish school.
ClassCover Standard is a flat annual fee scaled to your school's enrolment — from €600/year for schools up to 200 students, capped at €3,300/year for schools over 1,500 students. DEIS schools get a discount, and list prices are exclusive of VAT. ClassCover Lite stays free permanently for every Irish school. See the full pricing breakdown →
Standard lets you search your county for substitute teachers and SNAs your school does not know yet, and invite them onto your list. Schools joining now also get a direct feedback channel to our team.
ClassCover Standard is open in Cork, Dublin, Galway, Kildare, Limerick, Louth, Meath, Tipperary and Wicklow. Every other county starts on ClassCover Lite, free forever, and Standard opens as enough local substitute teachers and SNAs join.
The pilot ends on 30 October 2026. Pilot schools can keep ClassCover Standard from €600/year, or move to ClassCover Lite at no cost. Your list, your bookings and your history stay put either way. There is no obligation and no card on file. Annual fees scale with enrolment, with discounts for DEIS schools. See pricing →
Yes. ClassCover covers both substitute teachers and SNAs. Sub Seeker covers Teaching Council registered teachers only.
ClassCover doesn't carry out vetting, and it doesn't change who's responsible for it. Schools vet their SNAs and substitute teachers exactly as they do now, through their own vetting bodies. What ClassCover gives you is a list you curate and keep growing. You build it with the SNAs and subs you trust. A new person sits on a holding list until you confirm in the app that you've vetted them, and then they're yours to book. The difference is reach. Instead of one or two vetted SNAs you can call, you can connect with many more in your area, keep several moving through vetting at once, and build a list that gets deeper over time. ClassCover gives you the tools to manage that list and the connections to grow it.
