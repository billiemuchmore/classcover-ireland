A welcome from our founder

Fifteen years ago we started ClassCover in Australia with one belief: finding cover for a class should take minutes, not a morning of phone calls. Today, one in every two Australian schools relies on it.

The substitute crisis here is real, and it is well documented. We can't magic up new teachers. What we can do is take the scramble out of the morning, so the people you already trust are in one place, easy to reach, easy to book, and treated like the professionals they are.

That is what ClassCover gives you from your first day on it. Your own subs and SNAs, organised properly. Who is free, who is vetted, who said yes, all in writing. No group chats. No ringing down a list at ten to nine.

Thank you for putting your school forward. You are among the first schools in Ireland on ClassCover, and what you tell us over these first months shapes how it works for every school that follows.

Ben Grozier Founder & CEO, ClassCover

Why Standard opens county by county

Building the local network first, so schools have more people to choose from.

Finding the right substitute teacher or SNA is only useful when there are people available locally. That's why we build the network in each area first, bringing substitute teachers and SNAs on board before opening ClassCover Standard to schools. Once there's a strong enough pool of people nearby, schools in that county can start searching and finding the cover they need. Nine counties have reached this point already, and we're continuing to open up to schools across the country as the local network grows.

The earlier you join, the more you can get from Standard. Your school can start building its own list of trusted substitute teachers and SNAs from day one. As more people join in your area, your choice of available cover grows and the sooner Standard can open to your school.

More local people. More choice. Easier cover.
