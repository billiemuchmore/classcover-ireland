Three steps to fill an absence

No ringing round at seven in the morning. No group chats. No wondering who said yes.

1

Build your list

Add the subs and SNAs you already work with, and accept the requests to join that come in from local ones.

2

Send a booking request

Post the absence once. Everyone on your list who is free that day hears about it at the same moment.

3

Get an answer

The first available sub accepts and the booking is confirmed. You see who is coming, in writing.
