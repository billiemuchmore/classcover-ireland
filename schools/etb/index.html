Why ETBs need it

The approved list is out of date before it arrives.

01

Head office sends the approved list out as a spreadsheet. By the time it lands, many of the subs on it have already taken contracts.

02

How approval works

Approve once. Every school knows.

  1. 1

    The sub applies, or a school invites

    A sub applies to your ETB in the ClassCover app, or a school invites a sub it wants to use. The sub answers your ETB's questions.

  2. 2

    Head office reviews

    Every application lands in head office's own view, sorted by status. Head office runs its checks, then approves the sub or sends it back with a note.

  3. 3

    Every school can book

    Approved subs show a tick in every school in your ETB. Schools see who's free and book them in the app.

When an application needs more

Needs fixing

The sub is emailed to update it.

Under review

The application needs a closer look before a decision.

Unsuitable

The sub can't be booked in your ETB's schools.

Head office gives a green or orange tick, and your ETB decides what each one means.
