ClassCover Standard will open in your county once enough substitute teachers and SNAs have joined ClassCover.

1

What it does

Find more of the right people for your school.

Search your county for substitute teachers and SNAs who are not yet on your school's list, and invite them to join your ClassCover network.

2

Why the wait?

A bigger local network means a more useful service for schools.

ClassCover Standard works best when there is a strong pool of local substitute teachers and SNAs to connect with. That's why we're building the network county by county, ensuring there are enough people to find when the service opens.

3

When will it be available?

As soon as your county has the numbers.

We're opening ClassCover Standard county by county across Ireland as the local network grows.
