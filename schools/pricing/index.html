Questions

Pricing FAQs

Schools with up to 100 students pay €350 a year for ClassCover Standard, or €280 with the DEIS rate. Schools with 101 to 150 students pay €450. ClassCover Lite is free for every school.

DEIS primary and post-primary schools save 20% on ClassCover Standard in every enrolment band. Turn on "My school is DEIS" in the calculator above to see your price.

ClassCover Lite is free for every school in Ireland, in every county. ClassCover Standard opens first in Cork, Dublin, Galway, Kildare, Limerick, Louth, Meath, Tipperary and Wicklow, then in other counties once enough local substitute teachers and SNAs have joined ClassCover.

Lite lets your school upload its existing list of substitute teachers and SNAs and book them through the app, for free. Standard adds the ability to search your county for subs and SNAs your school does not know yet and invite them onto your list. It also keeps unlimited bookings and accepting join requests permanently, where those are launch specials on Lite.

Yes. Every price on this page includes Irish VAT. The price you see is the price you pay, with nothing added at checkout.

ClassCover Standard is billed annually, in advance, with the first charge on 2 December 2026. Invoice payment terms are 30 days. ETBs and multi-school groups can request custom billing arrangements from our team.

We use your enrolment at renewal to set the band for the following year. Your band stays the same for the whole school year.
